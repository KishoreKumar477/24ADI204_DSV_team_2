## **Project Abstract**

This project addresses the critical business challenge of customer churn—the phenomenon where customers cease their relationship with a service provider or product. In modern competitive landscapes, the cost of acquiring a new customer significantly outweighs the cost of retaining an existing one, making accurate churn prediction a vital component of sustainable growth. The core objective of this research is to develop a robust machine learning pipeline capable of identifying high-risk customers before they depart, allowing for proactive intervention and personalized retention strategies.

The methodology utilizes historical transactional and behavioral datasets to perform comprehensive feature engineering and statistical analysis. Recognizing that churn datasets are often highly imbalanced, the project employs specialized sampling techniques and advanced classification models, such as XGBoost and Random Forest, to achieve high precision and recall. Beyond simple binary classification, the project emphasizes model interpretability to uncover the underlying drivers of churn—such as service quality, pricing sensitivity, or engagement levels—transforming raw data into actionable business intelligence.

The uniqueness of this approach lies in its integration of descriptive analytics with predictive modeling, offering a holistic view of the customer lifecycle. By visualizing key performance indicators and risk scores, the project provides stakeholders with a clear narrative of customer health. The final implementation serves as a strategic tool for marketing and customer success teams, enabling data-driven decision-making that reduces churn rates and increases overall customer lifetime value.
